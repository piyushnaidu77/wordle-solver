How this works

Choose the secret word from the answer list. A reinforcement-learning policy picks every guess. It was trained to finish in as few guesses as possible, and each guess can be any allowed word.

Green means the letter is in the right spot. Yellow means it is in the word, in another spot. Gray means it is not in the word.
